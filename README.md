This repo contains the manifesto of the Free Palestine movement. It is merely a suggestion and does not reflect the views of any
individual, including its author. It is meant to be a framework and is the start of a negotiation.
There are some ideas that are up to the citizens of Israel to implement. No movement can demand a change in
leadership. It is up to the people to buy into the process.

It is important to note, that this document is external to Israel. It cannot be a demand, but part of a 3 way
conversation. Note that this document focuses on the people of Israel, Gaza, and the West Bank.
It preserves the end goal of removing threats caused by extremist groups whether they be religious extreme Zionist,
or religious extreme terror groups, or JIhadists.
